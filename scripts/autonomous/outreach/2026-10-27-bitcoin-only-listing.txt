Body:

Suggestion: BTC500 (https://btc500.net) for Explorers & Dashboards.

What it is: a Bitcoin-only research site built on one fixed rule — buy 500 days
before each halving, sell 500 days after — with four completed halving windows
laid out from a daily-close archive back to 2011. Each window shows the entry,
halving and exit close, the lowest close inside the window and the date it
happened, and a test of what missing the exact entry date by 30, 60 or 120 days
cost at the same exit.

No altcoins, no accounts, no ads, and the underlying numbers are also served as
JSON for anyone who wants to chart them.
