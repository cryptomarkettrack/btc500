BTC500 publishes the four completed Bitcoin halving 500-day windows (buy 500 days
before the halving, sell 500 days after): entry and exit date, multiple, the lowest
close against the entry, and the days from entry to the low.

- Worst post-entry drawdown: -83.65% (2012 window, $14.07 entry, low $2.30, 128 days
  from entry to the low).
- Median across the four windows: -11.72%.
- All four closed below their entry at some point after the buy (4 of 4).
- Next window opens 2026-11-28.

Numbers: https://btc500.net/data/500-day-cycles.json
Daily BTC/USD closes: https://btc500.net/btc-usd-max.csv
No sign-up, no ads, no affiliate or referral links, no custody of funds.
