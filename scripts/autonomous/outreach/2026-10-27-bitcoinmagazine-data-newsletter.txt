THE PITCH
Subject: In every completed 500-day window, Bitcoin closed below the entry price

One number from a daily-close archive that starts in July 2011: across every
completed 500-day window around a Bitcoin halving, price closed a median 11.7%
below the 500-days-before entry at some point before the halving itself. The
2012 window is the outlier on the deep side, and the page publishes each
window's lowest close and the date it happened, plus a test of what moving the
entry date 30, 60 and 120 days earlier or later did to the exit multiple.
Everything is computed from the same archive, daily closes only, and it is free
to cite.
